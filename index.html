Our Training Programs

Expert instruction tailored to every fencing stance, grip, and tactical strategy.

Foil (Fioretto)

The core training discipline focusing on point-precision, fast technical thrusts, and strict tactical right-of-way rules.

Epee (Spada)

A discipline of patience and timing where the entire body is a valid target area and matches feature simultaneous scoring touches.

Youth & Beginners

Our foundational courses introduce child athletes and adult beginners to core balance, footwork, and reflex control exercises.
